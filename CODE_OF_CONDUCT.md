# ini_manager community conduct

Project discussions, reviews and contributions should be useful and respectful.
Discuss code and observable behavior; give others room to ask questions, disagree
and correct mistakes. Personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, threats, unwanted
sexual attention and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive contributions and restrict participation
when needed to keep project spaces usable. They should explain their decisions
and consider the context and impact of the behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to LineGM@yandex.ru. Include enough context to
identify the incident. Reports should be handled discreetly and shared only with
people who need the information to resolve the concern. These expectations apply
to everyone participating in the project's spaces.
